Harvest Tapestry Theme brings a late-summer harvest to VS Code: cream paper and slate ink, sage green, pressed-flower pink and a thread of ochre. Two coordinated variants ship in one extension - a light theme with a deep slate-green status bar for daytime, and a dark theme on charcoal slate with a warm cream band for the evening. Syntax colors are woven from the same palette: teal strings, pale-ochre keywords, amber numbers and mauve types, each kept readable on its own background. Semantic highlighting, the full terminal ANSI palette, Git decorations, search and selection colors are all themed, so the editor, side bar, panels and terminal read as one piece.
